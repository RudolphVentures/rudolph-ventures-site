Turn geopolitical uncertainty into competitive advantage

Rudolph Ventures is a Brussels-based strategic advisory and media company led by Trevor H. Rudolph, whose career spans the White House, the technology industry, and the Fortune 500 business world. As a former government official and global government affairs executive, Trevor has crafted government policy, helped build a startup, and counseled multinational businesses on how to navigate geopolitical tensions between great powers.

Advisory

Strategic counsel to boards, executives, and associations on how to turn geopolitical and regulatory uncertainty into competitive advantage.

  • Regulatory and legislative strategyThe “Regulatory Law of Motion” tells us that any regulatory action in one region will see an equal and opposite reaction in competing regions of the world. Design your firm’s regulatory strategy for an uncertain world.
  • Advocacy strategy and campaignsBuild localized advocacy strategies that establish your brand reputation around local jobs, local suppliers, and local economic impact.
  • Geopolitical and supply chain risk managementLearn how to demonstrate “digital sovereignty” within a global marketplace. Understand how to navigate U.S. Entity List designations while staying off China’s Unreliable Entity List. Ensure your operations and suppliers do not open your firm to reputational, legal, and commercial risks.
  • Crisis management and reputational managementWhen a breach or regulatory shock hits, understand who needs to hear what, from whom, and when, while addressing both public and nonpublic information about your firm.

About

Trevor H. Rudolph is a global technology and cybersecurity policy executive whose career spans North America, Europe, and Asia. He currently serves as a Senior Non-Resident Fellow at the Atlantic Council GeoTech Center, and previously served as Schneider Electric’s first Vice President for Global Digital Policy & Regulation, Chief Operating Officer of cybersecurity startup WhiteHawk, and Chief of the White House’s Cyber and National Security Unit at OMB, where he architected the President’s Cybersecurity National Action Plan. A two-time Federal 100 Award winner and CISSP, he holds degrees from the College of William & Mary and Georgetown University.
